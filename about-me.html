About Me

Hello everyone! My name is Bryce Davis and I am so thankful that you have taken time out of your day to experience my website. I am currently a computer science student at the University of West Georgia. I began recreational programming at a young age where I grew an early passion and became intrigued with working with computers. However, with all that said, my true passion is music.

I began my music through the Heard County School System and have been a musician since late 2019 where I have focused a majority of my learning through percussional studies ranging from keyboard, auxiliary, rudimental drumming, and vocal expertise. At the Heard County Comprehensive High School, I participated in the Heard County Marching Braves in roles such as publicity officer and band co-captain with honors such as a John Philip Sousa Award recipient, multiple silver division titles, and a contributor to Heard County Drumline's Top 3 Southeastern Regional Percussion title. I have continued my studies at the University of West Georgia, participating in the West Georgia Marching Wolves as a snare drummer.

As stated previously, my passion is music as I have presented skills in instruction, composition, and performance throughout my career as a musician. However, I also hold talent as a singer and songwriter, presenting two released songs at the moment with acoustic-based indie music. After my studies, I hope to continue instruction at the advanced level part-time, using my degree and separate passion of computer programming to become a software engineer.

My teaching philosophy is simple and commonly approached among many professional percussion teachers. Whether you are a professional, intermediate, or pure beginner, the first thing that should always be approached is the basics. This allows all students to learn at the root and improve minuscule behaviors that may have been avoided in a separate teaching, which then leads to major changes/improvements. Then, the rest should be left up to comfortability and challenge. Music development is at different scales depending on the student, so each student should approach the sticks differently to what is comfortable to them, yet still challenges the level they are at.

Overall, I hope for this website to serve as a true resource for students who desire learning rudimental drumming to use in order to develop new skills. For those interested in the computing side, this is to serve as an example of the capabilities that basic HTML, JavaScript, and CSS can bring to a creator. For professionals who come across this website, I hope that this can be a showcase and, through my hard work, can help you determine if I am fit as your partner in composition, instruction, or performance.
