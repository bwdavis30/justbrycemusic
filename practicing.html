Practicing

Now that you have finished learning some of the fundamentals of rudiment drumming such as learning the technique in how to hold your sticks, the basics of how to read drum notation, and common rhythms and rudiments seen in rudimental drumming, it is now time to put those to practice. Practice can be approached in multiple ways.

First, the least formal way to practice, but still extremely relavent and beneficial to advancing your skills, is to put your skills to the drum. Whether it be an actual drum or drum pad, practicing by simply taking skills in your own hands can be very efficient in developing aspects in which you need to learn.

The other way to practice is to actually find resources to focus on specific skills or approaches to learning. While searching around for proper resources can be quite the task, this website offers a resources page that easily navigates some of the best resources for you. Whether you want to focus on specific rudiments, practice a specific instrument, or go ahead and begin learning your first ensemble piece, JustBryceMusic has you covered with plenty of resources to choose from to take your next steps in practicing. Make sure to go give them a look!

Before moving on to those resources, it cannot be stressed enough that the only true way to advance your skills in drumming is to either follow one of two methods. First, find a personal instructor to teach you one-on-one and guide you as you develop skills, ensuring that drumming is learned and apporached properly and to not miss out on any instruction that could be extremely important to your development as a drummer. Another method to ensure skills are being advanced properly is to join some type of music program. Whether it be a small group of like-mind musicians, a band, a drum corps, a local orchestra, joining some kind of group that surrounds yourself with those with common interests can highly influence you to continue those skills. After all, what's the point of developing a skill if you don't have a community to back you?

As final regards, thank you so much for learning, and make sure to explore the rest of the website, as it greatly benefits not only the creator, but your opportunities to better yourself in rudimental drumming.
